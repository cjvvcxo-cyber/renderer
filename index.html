Why rendering is split into two stages

      
Stage 1 creates the animation as a WebM master using the browser canvas. Stage 2 uses FFmpeg WebAssembly to convert the finished master to H.264 MP4. The WebM remains downloadable even if MP4 conversion cannot complete because of browser memory limits.
